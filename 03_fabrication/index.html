Week 3: Fabrication

Infinte 67

I utlized my CAD, laser cutting, and automoton skills together to complete the third week's assignment. We were tasked with using a motor and battery to createa a kinetic sculpture which is an artwork that uses kinetic movement. My kinetic sculpture is a model of a person holding a six and seven that raises up and down indefinetly, this is a take on the recent "six seven" meme.

Sketch vs Final Product

Infinite 67 in action

The movement of the Infinite 67 comes from using cams to transfer the motor's rotational movement into linear movement pusing the 6 and 7 up and creating negative space in the cam that allows gravity to pull the 6 and 7 back down. The cams are attatchted to their own individual gears that connect to a central gear connected to the motor.
